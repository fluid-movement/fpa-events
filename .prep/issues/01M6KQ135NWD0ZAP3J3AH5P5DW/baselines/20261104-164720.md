On the event manage (admin) area, when the event's city has no crew, show a small card: 'This city doesn't have a crew yet, want to start one?' linking to crew creation with the city prefilled from the event. Shown only to event managers, and hidden when the viewer is already in a crew or the city already has one. Dismissible, with the dismissal remembered the way SetupChecklist persists it.
