Users can create a crew: a community homepage for the jam in one city. Members and roles, a banner picture, one body text with a declared language that visitors can translate, contact links, regular jams, fun stats, and a list of all upcoming and past events in the crew's city. Public listing with a map. See the child issues for the detailed requirements.
