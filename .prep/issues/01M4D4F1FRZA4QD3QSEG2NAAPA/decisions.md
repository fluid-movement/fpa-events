## D1: No legal notice, no EU representative
date: 2026-10-08

The FPA is a US nonprofit, so the German Impressum duty does not reach it; the page is removed instead of filled in. An Art. 27 representative is arguably required for an ongoing service to EU users, but the user accepted that risk for a small non-commercial site. Alternative considered: a "Contact" page in place of the legal notice — not needed, since the privacy policy carries the contact address.
