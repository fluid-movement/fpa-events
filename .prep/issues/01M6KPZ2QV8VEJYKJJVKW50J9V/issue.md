Admins describe recurring jams: weekday, start time, a location, and an optional free-text note (e.g. 'first Friday of the month'). Location is a venue name with optional address and map position, reusing the venue picker pattern from schedules (VenueLocationPicker). Time zone comes from the crew's city. Shown on the crew page; usable for a 'next jam' badge on the listing. A crew can have several recurring jams.
