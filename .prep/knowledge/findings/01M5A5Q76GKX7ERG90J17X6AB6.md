title: Invite tokens are ULIDs with 80 random bits; calendar tokens are UUIDv4
description: ulid 3.0.2 draws each of the 16 random characters from crypto.getRandomValues, one byte scaled to 32 symbols (256/32 is exact, so there is no bias), giving 80 bits. The 48-bit time prefix is public and adds nothing. That is enough for a 48-hour, regenerable invite. Calendar tokens are crypto.randomUUID (122 bits) with no expiry, rotated only by the user. Neither needs changing; a future long-lived token should use randomUUID or 128+ bits, not a ULID.
